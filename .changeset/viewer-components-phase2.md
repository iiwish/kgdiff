The viewer's layout can be arranged, and its parts placed one by one. Every region of `<kgdiff-viewer>` (the change list, the detail panel, the search, the filters, the view dock, the replay transport, the legend, the zoom buttons, the minimap, the title, the actions) is open, collapsed or hidden through one attribute, `panels="detail:collapsed minimap:hidden"`, or the property `viewer.panels = { detail: 'collapsed' }` (reading it gives the page's declaration back; the new read-only `viewer.layout` has every region's state and the sizes); `sidebar="closed"`, `header="compact"` and `chrome="none"` keep working as presets of it. The detail panel can now be collapsed to a bar of its own, the sidebar's width and the split between the change list and the details can be dragged (or moved with the arrow keys), and `kgdiff-layout` reports every change. The viewer remembers what the reader changes (panels, sizes, theme, palette, language) in `localStorage` for the next visit, unless the page sets them or `persist="none"`; `persist-key` gives an app on a shared origin a store of its own. `shortcuts="scoped|global|none"` says where keyboard shortcuts apply (`global-shortcuts` still works) and `keymap="x:none r:review"` rebinds single keys (a letter in both cases, so Shift+J / K now step through the changes too); the `kgdiff-*` events are typed on every element, the document and the window (`KgdiffEventMap`; `KgdiffViewerEventMap` stays as a deprecated alias); `refreshTheme()` redraws the canvas after the page changed `--kgdiff-*` tokens, which are now documented, public CSS custom properties. Four of them are renamed to whole words before they become public: `--kgdiff-bg` → `--kgdiff-background`, `--kgdiff-mono` → `--kgdiff-font-mono`, `--kgdiff-text-2` → `--kgdiff-text-secondary` and `--kgdiff-text-3` → `--kgdiff-text-muted`; a page that set the old names sets the new ones instead. New, experimental elements compose a layout of your own: `<kgdiff-root>` holds the session, and `<kgdiff-stage>` (with five slots for your content over the canvas, which the graph is framed clear of, and `panels` for its own chrome: dock, transport, legend, zoom, minimap), `<kgdiff-change-list>`, `<kgdiff-change-detail>`, `<kgdiff-search>` and `<kgdiff-filters>` show its parts, inside it or anywhere on the page with `for="<root id>"`. `@kgdiff/viewer/elements/<name>` defines a single element, and `@kgdiff/viewer/panels` is the panels syntax (no DOM). The CLI's `--panels` sets a report's initial panel states; reports now carry their theme and language in the payload only, so a reader's earlier choice wins over them.
