Layers: an application draws its own marks on the renderer's canvas, under and over the graph (RFC 0003, part 4a; experimental). `renderer.addLayer({ order, draw(ctx, view) })` returns the function that removes the layer. An `under` layer is drawn after the background and the dot grid and before everything else, an `over` layer after the labels: a background behind a group of nodes, a certification mark or a count on a node, without a second canvas that follows the `camera` event. `draw` is called once per painted frame (once per side in a swipe view, clipped to it, with `view.side`), in CSS pixels, with `view.worldToScreen`, `view.camera`, `view.viewport` and `view.pixelRatio`; the renderer saves the context before each layer and restores it after. Layers are in `toDataURL` and in the `frame` event's `ms`, are not picked, and their text takes no part in label placement; a layer that throws is reported and the rest of the frame is painted. Without layers the renderer paints exactly as before. `<kgdiff-stage>` gains the same `addLayer` method (experimental) for the graph inside the viewer: layers added before the stage has a canvas are drawn once it has one, and they stay with the stage when it is bound to another root or moved in the document. `@kgdiff/viewer` exports the `RendererLayer` and `LayerView` types. Known limit: labels carry a halo in the canvas background color, which shows as a faint outline over a tinted `under` area; soft tints (the theme's `soft` colors, about 10% alpha) keep it unnoticeable.
