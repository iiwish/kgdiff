HTML reports are smaller: they embed the diff packed (`packDiffJSON`, format `kgdiff.diff.packed`, schema `schema/diff-packed.v1.json`), with its unchanged elements, which are most of a large diff, stored as tables of indices into one string table. For 100k nodes / 300k edges the embedded diff goes from 68.7 MB to 19.3 MB, 6.8 MB to 3.2 MB gzipped, and the report opens as fast. `unpackDiffJSON` gives the `DiffJSON` back byte for byte; the viewer reads packed and unpacked payloads (`ReportPayload.diff` is either), so older reports still open. The standalone viewer bundle budget goes from 100 kB to 105 kB (min + gzip) for the unpacking and the consistency checks of `diffFromJSON`.
