'@kgdiff/viewer': minor
---

Several views of one diff, and the canvas chrome as elements (experimental). A `<kgdiff-root>` now draws every `<kgdiff-stage>` bound to it, each with its own canvas, camera, zoom buttons and minimap, all sharing the selection, the hover, the filters, the search and the replay; `version="before"` or `version="after"` pins a stage to one version, for before and after side by side. The view dock, the replay transport, the legend, the zoom buttons, the minimap, the title and the actions are elements of their own (each says which root it shows with `rootElement`): `<kgdiff-view-dock>`, `<kgdiff-transport>`, `<kgdiff-legend>` (`variant="full|mini"`, `open`), `<kgdiff-zoom>` and `<kgdiff-minimap>` (bound to the nearest stage or to the one `stage="<id>"` names: `stage`, `stageElement`, `rootElement`; the minimap is in the custom state `:state(overview)` while the whole graph is in view), `<kgdiff-title>` and `<kgdiff-actions>`, each with a subpath `@kgdiff/viewer/elements/<name>`. Slotted into a stage, one of them replaces the built-in region while the stage's `panels` keeps applying to the rest; placed anywhere else it is no obstacle to fits and flights unless registered with `registerObstacle`. `<kgdiff-viewer>` gets the stage's five slots: your content in `slot="top-end"` (and `top-start`, `bottom-start`, `bottom-center`, `bottom-end`) goes over the canvas and the graph is framed clear of it. The shortcuts dialog lists the keys bound after `keymap`, and `?` in a composed root opens the dialog of its `<kgdiff-actions>`. Accessibility: the zoom toolbar, the appearance menu and the search's results have names, a `<kgdiff-stage>` is a named region rather than a main landmark, the end of a replay is announced once, and the page's content in the top corners comes first in the tab order. `@kgdiff/viewer/elements/root` no longer carries the renderer (64 kB instead of 88 kB, min + gzip): each stage brings its own.
